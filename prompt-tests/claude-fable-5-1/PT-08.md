# ARTIFACT 3: Recipe Drafts

## Dish 1: Coconut-Lime Chicken with Peas (mild)
**Serves 4**

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb / 450 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2.5 cm / 1 in pieces
- 1 tsp salt (for the chicken) + more to taste
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated or finely chopped (extra)
- 1 can coconut milk (400 ml / 13.5 oz)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 cup / 130 g frozen peas (straight from the freezer)
- Juice of 1 lime, plus 1 lime cut into wedges for the table
- 2 scallions, sliced, or a small handful of cilantro (extra, optional)
- Chili flakes, in a small dish at the table

**Method**
1. Pat chicken dry, toss with 1 tsp salt.
2. Heat oil in the large skillet over medium-high. Add chicken in one layer; don't move it for 3 min, then stir and cook 2 min more until browned outside (doesn't need to be cooked through yet). Remove to a plate.
3. Lower heat to medium. Add onion with a pinch of salt; cook 4–5 min until soft. Add garlic and ginger, stir 1 min.
4. Pour in coconut milk and soy sauce, scrape up the browned bits. Return chicken and any juices. Simmer gently (small bubbles, not a boil) 8–10 min until the chicken reaches **74°C / 165°F** on an instant-read thermometer, or is opaque all the way through with no pink.
5. Stir in peas; simmer 2 min. Turn off heat. Add lime juice. Taste: if it tastes flat, add salt or a splash more soy; if heavy, more lime.
6. Top with scallions/cilantro. Serve chili flakes and lime wedges separately so each person adds their own heat.

**Balance notes**
- *Salt:* soy sauce + salt on the chicken and onion. Coconut milk is unsalted, so taste at the end.
- *Fat:* coconut milk and chicken-thigh fat carry the flavor.
- *Acid:* lime juice at the very end (added early it goes dull); lime wedges at the table.
- *Heat (spice):* zero in the pot; chili flakes per plate for adults and the brave 9-year-old. *Heat (cooking):* hard sear for browning, then a gentle simmer so the thighs stay tender and the coconut milk doesn't split.

**Food safety:** chicken to 74°C / 165°F. Wash hands, board, and knife after handling raw chicken.

## Dish 2: Roasted Broccoli
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets; stem peeled and sliced into coins
- 1½ tbsp oil, ½ tsp salt

Toss on the sheet pan, spread out, roast at 220°C / 425°F for 15–18 min until edges brown. Squeeze a little lime over at the end.

**Balance:** salt on the florets before roasting; oil for browning and richness; lime squeeze for acid; high oven heat for crisp edges (crowded or wet broccoli steams instead, so dry it and spread it out).

## Dish 3: Jasmine Rice
- 2 cups jasmine rice, 2½ cups / 600 ml water, ½ tsp salt

Rinse rice in the saucepan until water runs mostly clear; drain. Add water and salt, bring to a boil, cover, drop to lowest heat, 12 min. Off heat, lid on, rest 10 min. Fluff.

**Balance:** salt in the water (plain rice under a salted sauce works fine, but a little salt helps); fat and acid come from the sauce and lime on top; heat: low and covered, no peeking.
